Verifying your details is an important step to make your studio <strong>“Verified”</strong> on Beat22. Follow the guide below to complete both <strong>Mobile Number Verification</strong> and <strong>Aadhaar Verification via DigiLocker</strong> easily.
<h3><strong>Step 1: Mobile Number Verification</strong></h3>
<ol>
 	<li>Go to <strong>Settings → Account Settings</strong> in your Beat22 dashboard.</li>
 	<li>Enter your <strong>mobile number</strong> in the given field.</li>
 	<li>Click on <strong>Verify</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-602 size-full" src="/images/2025/11/Screenshot-2025-11-05-173348.png" alt="" width="737" height="145" />

4. A <strong>popup</strong> will appear asking for the OTP sent to your number.

5. Enter the <strong>OTP</strong> and click on <strong>Submit</strong>.

6. Once verified, your mobile number will show as <strong>Verified</strong>.
<h3><strong>Step 2: Aadhaar Verification (via DigiLocker)</strong></h3>
<h4><strong>If you’re 18 or above:</strong></h4>
<ol>
 	<li>Under Account Settings, go to <strong>Aadhaar Verification</strong>.</li>
 	<li>Click on <strong>Verify via DigiLocker</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-603" src="/images/2025/11/Screenshot-2025-11-05-153506.png" alt="" width="1003" height="624" />

3. A popup will appear — tick the checkbox for <strong>“I have read and agree to the Terms &amp; Conditions.”</strong>

4. Click on <strong>Continue with DigiLocker</strong>.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-604" src="/images/2025/11/Screenshot-2025-11-05-153907.png" alt="" width="1205" height="643" />

5. You’ll be redirected to the DigiLocker page.

6. Click on <strong>Start Verification</strong> when prompted.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-605" src="/images/2025/11/Screenshot-2025-11-05-153931.png" alt="" width="957" height="867" />

7. On the next page, tick <strong>“I consent to terms of use.”</strong>

8. Click on <strong>Sign In</strong>.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-616" src="/images/2025/11/Screenshot-2025-11-07-125358.png" alt="" width="722" height="630" />

9. You’ll receive an <strong>OTP on your registered mobile number</strong>, enter it and click <strong>Sign In</strong> again.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-617" src="/images/2025/11/Screenshot-2025-11-07-125443.png" alt="" width="718" height="562" />

10. A final DigiLocker popup will appear. Tick on <strong>Aadhaar Card</strong> and click <strong>Allow</strong>.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-618" src="/images/2025/11/Screenshot-2025-11-07-125719.png" alt="" width="734" height="648" />

11. You’ll see a message that says <strong>“Redirecting back to requester.”</strong>

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-609" src="/images/2025/11/Screenshot-2025-11-05-154141.png" alt="" width="1141" height="624" />

12. Once redirected, the data will be fetched automatically.

13. <strong>Refresh the page</strong>, and you’ll see your Aadhaar status as <strong>Verified.</strong>
<h4><strong>If you’re below 18:</strong></h4>
You’ll need to complete <strong>two verifications</strong>, first with <strong>your own Aadhaar</strong>, and then again with your <strong>guardian’s Aadhaar</strong> following the same DigiLocker steps above.

<strong>Done!</strong>

Once both your <strong>mobile number</strong> and <strong>Aadhaar</strong> are verified, your studio will officially be marked as <strong>Verified on Beat22</strong>, unlocking better visibility, higher trust, and access to full platform features.
