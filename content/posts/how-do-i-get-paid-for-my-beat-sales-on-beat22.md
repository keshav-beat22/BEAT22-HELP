If you're a music producer selling beats on Beat22, getting paid is easy and transparent. Here's everything you need to know about how payments work:
<h3><strong>1. Add your bank details</strong></h3>
To receive payouts, you need to link your <strong>bank account</strong> to your Beat22 seller profile.
Just go to <strong>Settings &gt; Payout Bank Details</strong> and fill in your accurate account information.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-222 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-233724.png" alt="" width="1129" height="377" />

Make sure the details are correct to avoid payout delays.
<h3><strong>2. When do you get paid?</strong></h3>
Once a buyer purchases your beat:
<ul>
 	<li>The payment is processed securely.</li>
 	<li>After deducting Beat22’s commission (for basic sellers), your share is scheduled for payout.</li>
 	<li><strong>Payouts are processed within 3–5 business days</strong> after a sale.</li>
</ul>
You’ll receive an email or SMS once the payout is successfully initiated.
<h3><strong>3. What about refunds or issues?</strong></h3>
If a buyer reports a problem or requests a refund (rare cases), the payout may be temporarily held until the issue is resolved.

But don’t worry, we’ll always keep you updated and protect your interests as a seller.
<h3><strong>Need help?</strong></h3>
If you haven’t received a payout or need help updating your bank details, just email us at <strong>support@beat22.com</strong>
<h3><strong>Final Tip: Keep uploading and promoting</strong></h3>
The more active you are, the higher your chances of making consistent sales and building your brand. Beat22 is here to support your journey every step of the way.
