Your <strong>Display Picture (DP)</strong> is the first thing buyers and artists see on your profile — so make it count! Updating it is quick and easy.
<h2><strong>Steps to change your display picture:</strong></h2>
<ol>
 	<li>1. Go to your <strong><strong>Settings</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-267 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-01-004741.png" alt="" width="971" height="207" />

&nbsp;</li>
 	<li>Click on the <strong>Studio Setup</strong> tab</li>
 	<li>Click on the <strong><strong>Profile Picture</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-274 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-01-005536.png" alt="" width="944" height="313" />

&nbsp;</li>
 	<li>Choose your image from your device</li>
 	<li>Click <strong>Submit</strong></li>
</ol>
Your new DP will be updated instantly across the platform.
<h2><strong>Tips for a Great DP:</strong></h2>
<ul>
 	<li>Use a <strong>clear, high-quality image</strong></li>
 	<li>Avoid logos with text that's too small to read</li>
 	<li>Show your face or brand identity — it builds trust!</li>
</ul>
