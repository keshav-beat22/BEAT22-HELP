<span style="color: #ffffff;">Need to update your billing address for invoices or purchases? On Beat22, you can easily <strong>delete and add a new one</strong>.</span>

Here’s how:

<strong>Step-by-Step: Update Your Billing Address</strong>
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Log in to Your Account</strong>
Go to <a href="https://beat22.com">beat22.com</a> and sign in with your buyer account.</li>
 	<li><strong>Click on Your Profile Icon (Top Right Corner)</strong>
Once logged in, click your profile icon at the top-right of the page.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-352 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-184159.png" alt="" width="1156" height="106" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Go to “Settings”</strong>
From the dropdown menu, select <strong>“Settings”</strong> to open your account settings.<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-353 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-184217.png" alt="" width="1161" height="202" /></li>
 	<li><strong><strong><strong><strong>Find the “Billing Address” Section
Scroll to the Billing Address area.</strong></strong></strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-369 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-191644.png" alt="" width="934" height="248" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Click the Trash Icon </strong><strong>🗑️
To remove your existing address, click the dustbin icon next to it.</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>Add a New Billing Address
You can keep the previous billing address and add a new billing address as well. Click “Add Address” and enter your new billing details.</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>Save Changes
Once entered, click Save to update.</strong></li>
</ol>
<h2><strong>Why This Matters:</strong></h2>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Keeps your invoices accurate</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>Ensures smooth payment processing</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>Helps with tax and legal compliance</strong></li>
</ul>
