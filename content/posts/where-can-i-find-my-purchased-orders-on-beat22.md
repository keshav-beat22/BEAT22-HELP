Once you’ve purchased a beat on Beat22, you can easily access your files including the license, audio files, invoice, and receipt, anytime from your account.

Follow these simple steps:
<h2><strong>How to access your purchased orders:</strong></h2>
<strong>1. Log in to your Beat22 account</strong>
Visit <a href="https://beat22.com">beat22.com</a> and sign in with your buyer account.

<strong>2. Click on “Browse” at the top</strong>
Once logged in, you’ll see the <strong>“Browse”</strong> button at the top of the page. Click on it.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-309 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-152726.png" alt="" width="1158" height="130" />

<strong>3. Go to “My Orders”</strong>
In the dropdown, click on <strong>“My Orders”</strong>. This will open your full order history.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-310 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-152740.png" alt="" width="1159" height="219" />

<strong>4. Select the Beat You Purchased</strong>
Find the beat you’ve purchased from the list and click on it.

<strong>5. Download Your Files</strong>
From the beat’s order details page, you’ll be able to:
<ul>
 	<li>Download the <strong>media file</strong> (MP3/WAV/Stems)</li>
 	<li>Download the <strong>license PDF</strong></li>
 	<li>Access your <strong>invoice and payment receipt</strong></li>
</ul>
<strong>Quick Tip:</strong>

Your files will always remain available in your account under “My Orders.” You can return any time to re-download if needed.
