Need to update your password for security or personal reasons? No problem — Beat22 makes it quick and easy to change your password directly from your account settings.

Follow this step-by-step guide:
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Log in to Your Account</strong>
Visit <a href="https://beat22.com">beat22.com</a> and sign in with your current email and password.</li>
 	<li><strong>Click on Your Profile Icon (Top Right Corner)</strong>
After logging in, click on your <strong>profile icon</strong> at the top-right of the page.<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-352 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-184159.png" alt="" width="1156" height="106" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Go to “Settings”</strong>
In the dropdown menu, select <strong>“Settings”</strong> to open your account preferences.<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-353 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-184217.png" alt="" width="1161" height="202" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Scroll to the “Change Password” section</strong>
You’ll find a section labeled <strong>Change Password</strong>.<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-359 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-185806.png" alt="" width="1062" height="171" /></li>
 	<li><strong><strong>Enter the required details
</strong></strong>Enter your <strong>current password. </strong>Enter your <strong>new password. </strong>Confirm your <strong><strong>new password</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-360 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-185916.png" alt="" width="1058" height="430" />

&nbsp;</li>
 	<li><strong>Click “Change Password”</strong></li>
</ol>
Once updated, your password will be changed instantly and securely.

<strong>Forgot Your Password?</strong>

If you’re locked out of your account or can’t remember your password, go to the <strong>Login Page</strong> and click <strong>“Forgot Password”</strong>. We’ll send you a reset link via email.

<strong>Pro Tip:</strong>

Use a strong password that includes letters, numbers, and symbols for maximum security.
