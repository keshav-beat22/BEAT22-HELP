Becoming a seller on Beat22 is simple, but you need to set up your account properly before you can start selling beats and receiving payouts. Follow this detailed guide to complete your setup once and for all.
<h3><strong>Step 1: Login to Your Seller Dashboard</strong></h3>
<ol>
 	<li>Go to <strong>Beat22.com</strong>.</li>
 	<li><strong>Sign Up</strong>
Start by creating your account on Beat22.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-582" src="/images/2025/09/how-to-buy-1.png" alt="" width="283" height="415" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Verify Your Email</strong>
After signing up, you’ll be asked to enter the <strong>OTP</strong> sent to your registered email. Complete this step to verify your account.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-583" src="/images/2025/09/how-to-buy-2.png" alt="" width="468" height="289" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Select Seller Option</strong>
Once your email is verified, a pop-up will appear asking if you’re a music producer. Select <strong>“Yes, I want to sell.”
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-585" src="/images/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-08-183726.png" alt="" width="683" height="390" />
</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>Submit Work Links</strong>
Another pop-up will then ask you to share your work links. Provide:
<ul>
 	<li>A link to any project or music you’ve worked on.</li>
 	<li>Additionally, your <strong>Instagram</strong> or <strong>YouTube</strong> profile link (if available).
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-586" src="/images/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-08-183844.png" alt="" width="600" height="639" /></li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li><strong>Click Submit</strong>
After entering the details, click <strong>Submit</strong>. Your seller approval usually takes <strong>1–3 hours</strong>.</li>
 	<li><strong>Set Up Your Seller Account</strong>
While waiting for approval, you can start setting up your seller profile by going to <strong>Settings </strong>tab in your dashboard<strong>.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-587" src="/images/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-08-183937.png" alt="" width="536" height="300" /></strong></li>
</ol>
Here, you will see <strong>5 sections on the left side:</strong>
<ul>
 	<li>Account</li>
 	<li>Payout Bank Details</li>
 	<li>Studio Setup</li>
 	<li>Studio Controls</li>
 	<li>Manage Subscription
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-588" src="/images/2025/09/how-to-buiy-6.png" alt="" width="326" height="288" /></li>
</ul>
You need to complete each of these one by one.
<h3><strong>Step 2: Account Details</strong></h3>
Click on <strong>Account</strong> and fill in the following information:
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Under General Information:</strong></li>
</ol>
<ul>
 	<li>Verify the mobile number.</li>
 	<li>Verify Aadhaar card. To learn to verify mobile no. and Aadhaar card: <a href="/how-to-verify-your-mobile-number-and-aadhaar-on-beat22/">Click here</a>

<strong>2. Under Studio Information:</strong></li>
 	<li>Fill in the studio name. <a href="/how-can-i-change-my-studio-name/">Click here to know more</a></li>
 	<li>Fill in the studio handle. <a href="/how-can-i-change-my-studio-handle/">Click here to know more</a>
<strong>
3. Billing Address
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-590" src="/images/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-08-184207.png" alt="" width="679" height="356" />
</strong></li>
</ul>
<strong>Tip:</strong> Fill everything carefully and double-check spelling. Your <strong>Studio Handle</strong> especially matters, since it will appear in your Beat22 URL (e.g., beat22.com/@yourhandle).
<h3><strong>Step 3: Payout Bank Details</strong></h3>
Without this step, you cannot receive payments. Go to <strong>Payout Bank Details</strong> and add:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Account Holder’s Name</strong> (must match PAN/bank details)</li>
 	<li><strong>Bank Account Number</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>IFSC Code
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-591" src="/images/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-08-184236.png" alt="" width="632" height="352" />
</strong></li>
</ul>
Once saved, payouts will be sent directly to your bank account within <strong>3–5 business days</strong> of a sale.
<h3><strong>Step 4: Studio Setup</strong></h3>
This is where you create your <strong>public-facing profile</strong> (your “studio” on Beat22).

Fill out the following:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Display Picture (DP):</strong> Upload a clear profile photo (1:1 ratio).</li>
 	<li><strong>Account Info / Bio:</strong> A short introduction about you as a producer.</li>
 	<li><strong>Skills Summary:</strong> List what you do - beat making, mixing, mastering, production styles, etc.</li>
 	<li><strong>Spotify Embed Link:</strong> If you have music released, paste your Spotify embed link so buyers can hear your work.</li>
 	<li><strong>Social Media Links:</strong> Add all your socials - Instagram, YouTube, SoundCloud, etc.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-592" src="/images/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-08-184307.png" alt="" width="523" height="674" /></li>
</ul>
<strong>Pro Tip:</strong> Buyers trust complete profiles with links. It shows you are active and professional.
<h3><strong>Step 5: Studio Controls</strong></h3>
This is where you configure how your beats are sold.
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Global Negotiation Rule:</strong> Toggle this ON to enable negotiation on <strong>all your exclusive beats</strong>.</li>
</ul>
Highly recommended - this allows buyers to send you offers, making it easier to close sales.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-594" src="/images/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-08-184926.png" alt="" width="800" height="241" />
<h3><strong>Step 6: Manage Subscription</strong></h3>
Here you can view and manage your current seller plan (Basic, Premium, Pro Monthly, Pro Annual).
<ul>
 	<li>Upgrade if you want <strong>unlimited uploads, analytics, verified badge, coupon enrollment, placements, and more visibility.</strong> Click here to learn about subscriptions</li>
 	<li>Downgrade/cancel if needed.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-593" src="/images/2025/09/Screenshot-2025-09-08-184625.png" alt="" width="1087" height="292" />
<strong>
Final Step: Submit for Approval</strong></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li>Once you’ve completed <strong>all 5 slots</strong> - Account, Payout, Studio Setup, Studio Controls, and Subscription — your studio becomes fully functional and ready to go live.
<ul>
 	<li>After finishing setup, <strong>submit your profile for approval.</strong></li>
 	<li>Approval usually takes <strong>1–3 hours.</strong></li>
 	<li>You will receive an <strong>email notification</strong> once your account is either approved or rejected (with reasons).</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>Once approved, you can immediately start uploading your beats.
Click here to learn how: <a href="/how-to-upload-beats/"><strong><u>How to Upload Beats on Beat22</u></strong></a></li>
</ul>
Remember: The more complete and professional your setup, the more buyers will trust you and purchase your beats.
