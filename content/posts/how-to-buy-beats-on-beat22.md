Buying a beat on Beat22 is super simple. Here’s exactly how it works:
<h3><strong>Step-by-Step guide:</strong></h3>
<strong>1. Choose Your Beat</strong>
Browse the Beat22 library and find the beat you like.

<strong>2. Click the Price Button</strong>
Once you’ve decided, click the <strong>price button</strong> shown on the beat. This is also the <strong>"Buy" button</strong>.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-281 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-132221.png" alt="" width="1237" height="198" />

<strong>3. Select your license type</strong>
After clicking, you’ll be shown three options:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>WAV (MP3 + WAV files)</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>WAV + Stems (Full project-ready files)</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>Exclusive License (One-time, you own it)</strong></li>
</ul>
Click the price again under your preferred option.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-282 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-132242.png" alt="" width="1236" height="495" />

<strong>4. Beat added to cart</strong>
Once clicked, your beat is added to the cart automatically.

<strong>5. Go to cart &amp; apply coupons</strong>
Head over to the cart. If any valid <strong>coupon</strong> is active, it’ll be applied automatically.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-283 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-132302.png" alt="" width="1234" height="435" />

<strong>6. Checkout with your preferred payment method</strong>
Beat22 supports:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>UPI, Cards, Wallets, Net Banking</strong> (India)</li>
 	<li><strong>PayPal &amp; International Cards</strong> (Global)
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-673" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2026-02-14-142935-1.png" alt="" width="500" height="699" /></li>
</ul>
Make the payment and you’re done!
<h2><strong>Learn how to get beats after purchase: <a href="/2025/07/07/how-can-i-download-my-purchased-beats/">Click here</a></strong></h2>
<ul>
 	<li> Please click on “<strong>Browse</strong>” in the menu section</li>
 	<li>Then click on “<strong>Your Orders</strong>”</li>
 	<li>Now open the order, which contains the desired beat</li>
 	<li>Click the down arrow under “Actions” to download the beat files, or the down arrow under “License” to download the license document.</li>
 	<li>You also get a copy of everything in your <strong>email</strong>.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-672" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2026-02-14-150018.png" alt="" width="1675" height="382" /></li>
</ul>
<strong>Now you’re all set to record and release your song.</strong>
Got questions? Reach out at support@beat22.com anytime!
