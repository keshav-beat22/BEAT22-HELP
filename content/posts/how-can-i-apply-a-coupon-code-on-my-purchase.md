Good news, <strong>you don’t need to search for or ask anyone for a coupon code</strong> on Beat22!

If a seller has added a coupon to their beat, the system will <strong>automatically apply it</strong> at checkout. No manual entry. No extra steps.

<strong>Here’s How It Works:</strong>
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Pick a Beat</strong>
Browse and choose any beat you like on <a href="https://beat22.com">Beat22.com</a>.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-425 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-07-162456.png" alt="" width="1251" height="209" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Select a License Type</strong>
Click on the price next to WAV, WAV + Stems, or Exclusive to add the beat to your cart.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-426 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-07-162507.png" alt="" width="1252" height="505" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Open Your Cart</strong>
Click the cart icon at the top-right of the site to view your order.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-427 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-07-162518.png" alt="" width="1258" height="124" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Coupon Auto-Applies</strong>
If the seller has a coupon on that beat, the discount will <strong>automatically reflect</strong> in your cart.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-428 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-07-162529.png" alt="" width="1254" height="429" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Complete Payment</strong>
Just proceed to payment with the discounted total.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-429 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-07-162540.png" alt="" width="631" height="618" /></li>
</ol>
<strong>Important:</strong>
<ul>
 	<li>You <strong>do not need to enter any code manually</strong>.</li>
 	<li>If a beat has a coupon, it will apply <strong>automatically</strong>.</li>
 	<li>You <strong>don’t need to ask</strong> anyone for a code. If it’s available, you’ll see the discount right away.</li>
</ul>
Now you can focus on making music and saving smart.
