Trying to switch your payment method for your active Beat22 subscription? Here's what you need to know.
<h2><strong>Payment method can’t be changed mid-cycle</strong></h2>
If you've already subscribed using a payment method (e.g., credit card, UPI, etc.), that method is <strong>locked in</strong> for the upcoming automated renewal.

This means:
<ul>
 	<li>If your subscription is set to auto-renew, the system will <strong>use the same payment method</strong> again.</li>
 	<li>You <strong>cannot update or switch</strong> the payment method for the next renewal directly from your profile.</li>
</ul>
<strong>So, How Can You Use a New Payment Method?</strong>

To change your payment method, you’ll need to follow these steps:
<h2><strong>Step-by-Step: Switch to a new payment method</strong></h2>
<ol>
 	<li>
<h3><strong>Cancel your current subscription mandate</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li>Go to your <strong>subscription settings</strong></li>
 	<li>Cancel the existing <strong>auto-renewal mandate</strong></li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>
<h3><strong>Wait for the plan to expire</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li>Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>
<h3><strong>Resubscribe using the new payment method</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li>Once expired, go to the <strong>Subscription Plans</strong> page</li>
 	<li>Choose your preferred plan</li>
 	<li>At checkout, select your <strong>new payment method</strong></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<em>Tip: You can upgrade to Pro Annual during this step if you’re planning to change plans too!</em>
<h2><strong>Why this happens</strong></h2>
Beat22 uses secure third-party payment gateways that generate a <strong>billing mandate</strong> with your selected method. Once set, it can’t be changed for that cycle due to how auto-renewals are processed securely.
<h2><strong>FAQs</strong></h2>
<strong>Q: Can I change my payment method anytime?</strong>
A: Not during an active cycle. You can only change it by cancelling and resubscribing.

<strong>Q: Will I lose my subscription benefits if I cancel the mandate?</strong>
A: No. Your benefits will remain active until your current billing period ends.

<strong>Q: I don’t know how to cancel my mandate. What should I do?</strong>
A: Email us at <strong>support@beat22.com</strong>, and we’ll guide you step by step.
