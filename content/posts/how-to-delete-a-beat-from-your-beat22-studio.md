Want to remove a beat you uploaded by mistake or simply want to refresh your catalog? Deleting a beat from your Beat22 account is simple. Follow the steps below:
<h3><strong>Step-by-Step Guide</strong></h3>
<h4><strong>Step 1: Log into your Beat22 account</strong></h4>
Visit <a href="https://www.beat22.com">www.beat22.com</a> and log in using your producer’s credentials.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-184 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-205008.png" alt="" width="1083" height="517" />
<h4><strong>Step 2: Go to “My Beats”</strong></h4>
Once logged in, click your profile icon in the top-right corner and choose "My Beats" from the dropdown menu.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-185 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-205058.png" alt="" width="1092" height="269" />
<h4><strong>Step 3: Locate the beat you want to delete</strong></h4>
Browse or search to find the specific beat you want to delete from your list.
<h4><strong>Step 4: Click the delete icon</strong></h4>
You’ll see a trash bin icon next to each beat. Click it for the beat you want to remove.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-186 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-205112.png" alt="" width="1090" height="306" />
<h4><strong>Step 5: Confirm Deletion</strong></h4>
A confirmation popup will appear. Click "Yes" to remove the beat from your profile permanently.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-187 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-205127.png" alt="" width="1089" height="392" />
<h4> <strong><em>Important:</em></strong> Once deleted, the beat cannot be recovered. However, any licenses already sold will still remain valid.</h4>
<h3><strong>FAQs</strong></h3>
<strong>Q1. Can I recover a beat after deleting it?</strong>
No, once deleted, the beat is permanently removed from your profile.

<strong>Q2. What happens to buyers who purchased the beat before deletion?</strong>
Their licenses remain valid and downloadable as per the license agreement.

<strong>Q3. I don’t see a delete option. What should I do?</strong>
Please email us at <strong>support@beat22.com</strong> with your Beat ID and title. Our team will assist you.

Need more help? <strong>Contact us at support@beat22.com</strong>, we’re here to help you manage your catalog smoothly!
