Negotiations on Beat22 give you the flexibility to close deals your way. Here's how it works for sellers:
<h3><strong>Negotiation flow:</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li>You can <strong>accept</strong> the buyer's offer anytime.</li>
 	<li>You can also <strong>counter</strong> the offer, change the <strong>price</strong>, <strong>publishing rights</strong>, or <strong>master rights</strong>.</li>
 	<li>You can respond up to <strong>3 rounds</strong> (offer &amp; counter).</li>
</ul>

<h3><strong>Ending a negotiation:</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li>After 3 rounds of negotiation, you'll get the option to <strong>Accept</strong> or <strong>Reject</strong>.</li>
 	<li>If you don’t want to proceed further, you can simply reject at this point.</li>
</ul>
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-237 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-235643.png" alt="" width="1014" height="254" />
<h3><strong>Buyer checkout time:</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li>Once you accept an offer, the buyer has <strong>24 hours</strong> to complete the checkout.</li>
 	<li>If they don’t, the negotiation offer will <strong>automatically expire</strong>.</li>
</ul>
