Want to personalize your Beat22 account with your own profile photo? It’s super easy and takes just a few seconds!

Follow this step-by-step guide to update your profile picture:
<h2><strong>Step-by-Step: Update your profile picture</strong></h2>
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Log in to Your Account</strong>
Go to <a href="https://beat22.com">beat22.com</a> and sign in.</li>
 	<li><strong>Click on your profile icon (Top right corner)</strong>
Once logged in, you’ll see your profile icon at the top-right of the screen. Click on it to open the menu.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-352 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-184159.png" alt="" width="1156" height="106" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Go to “Settings”</strong>
From the dropdown, select <strong>“Settings”</strong> to open your account settings.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-353 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-184217.png" alt="" width="1161" height="202" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Click on the profile picture area</strong>
You’ll see your current profile photo (or a placeholder image). Click on it.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-354 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-184230.png" alt="" width="1160" height="409" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Upload a new picture</strong>
Select a photo from your device and upload it. Make sure the image is clear and appropriate.</li>
 	<li><strong>Save changes</strong>
Once uploaded, your profile picture will update automatically. You’re all set!</li>
</ol>
<strong>You can update it anytime</strong>

Want to change your photo again later? Just repeat the steps above.
