Made a sale on Beat22 but didn’t get the payment yet? Don’t worry, in most cases, there’s a simple reason behind it. Let’s walk you through everything you need to know.
<ol>
 	<li>
<h2><strong> Payments are not Instant</strong></h2>
</li>
</ol>
Seller payments are processed after a <strong>settlement period</strong>, not immediately after the sale.

<strong>Settlement Time:</strong> It usually takes <strong>15 days</strong> for payments to reflect in your bank account after a sale. To know more, <a href="https://blog.beat22.com/beat22-introduces-14-day-payment-system-strengthening-trust-and-transparency-for-sellers/">click here.</a>
<ol start="2">
 	<li>
<h2><strong> Payouts are scheduled in batches</strong></h2>
</li>
</ol>
We process payouts in batches through our payment partners. If your payout is in queue, it will be sent during the next payout cycle.

<em>Note:</em> Weekends and bank holidays can delay processing.
<ol start="3">
 	<li>
<h2><strong> Have you added/updated your bank details ?</strong></h2>
</li>
</ol>
Make sure your payout method is correctly set up.
<ul>
 	<li>Go to your <strong>Profile Settings</strong> → <strong>Payout Bank Details</strong></li>
 	<li>Add your <strong>Bank Details</strong></li>
 	<li>Ensure the details are accurate to avoid failed payouts</li>
</ul>
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-222 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-233724.png" alt="" width="1129" height="377" />
<ol start="4">
 	<li>
<h2><strong> Your payment has likely been initiated</strong></h2>
</li>
</ol>
If it’s been over 5 business days and your payout hasn't reached you, it might already be <strong>initiated but delayed</strong> by the bank/payment gateway. You can:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Check your email/SMS</strong> for payment notifications</li>
 	<li><strong>Wait another 24–48 hours</strong></li>
 	<li>Or <strong>contact our support team</strong> at support@beat22.com</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>FAQs</strong></h2>
<strong>Q: How often does Beat22 release payouts?</strong>
A: Every 15 days in regular cycles.

<strong>Q: Where can I see my earnings?</strong>
A: Go to your Browse → Studio Orders → Check "Payout Status."

<strong>Q: My payment failed. What should I do?</strong>
A: Double-check your payout details. If everything is correct, contact us with the screenshot of your sale.

<strong>Still need help?</strong>

Reach out to us anytime at <strong>support@beat22.com</strong> — we’re here to make sure you get paid smoothly.
