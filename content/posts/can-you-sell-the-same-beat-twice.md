<strong>Yes, you can, depending on the license.</strong>

Let’s break it down
<h2><strong>1. Selling with Non-Exclusive Licenses</strong></h2>
When you upload a beat, you can sell <strong>multiple non-exclusive licenses</strong> for it. This means:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Multiple artists can buy and use the same beat</strong></li>
 	<li>Each gets limited rights (based on license type: Basic, Premium, etc.)</li>
 	<li>You keep full ownership of the beat</li>
</ul>
<em>This is how most producers make consistent income by licensing the same beat to many buyers.</em>
<h2><strong>2. What happens with Exclusive License?</strong></h2>
If a buyer purchases the <strong>Exclusive License</strong>, then:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>You cannot sell that beat again to anyone else</strong> <strong>because it is taken down from the platform</strong></li>
 	<li>It’s a one-time sale with full rights handed over to the buyer</li>
 	<li>All previous non-exclusive buyers can still use it (unless stated otherwise)</li>
</ul>
<em>After an Exclusive License is sold, you must remove the beat from sale to avoid legal issues.</em>
<h3><strong>Pro tip for Sellers</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Want to maximize income?</strong> Keep selling non-exclusive until someone approaches for exclusivity.</li>
 	<li>Use the <strong>Negotiation feature</strong> on Beat22 to let serious buyers offer you a price for Exclusive rights. Use the link to know how</li>
</ul>
Selling the same beat twice is totally fine <strong>as long as you haven’t sold Exclusive rights yet</strong>. Use this to your advantage and build a catalog that keeps earning, <strong>beat after beat</strong>.
