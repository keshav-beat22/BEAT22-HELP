Nope, you don’t have to worry about that.

Once your beat is sold with an <strong>Exclusive License</strong> on Beat22, it is <strong>automatically taken down from the platform</strong>. The system handles it instantly; no manual action needed from your side.
<ol>
 	<li>It’s fully automated</li>
 	<li>No risk of double selling</li>
 	<li>You stay focused on creating</li>
</ol>
Beat22 takes care of the backend, so you don’t have to.
