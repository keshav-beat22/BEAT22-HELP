Your <strong>Studio Handle</strong> is your public identity on Beat22, visible on your beats, profile, and during buyer interactions. Want to change it? It’s super simple.
<h2><strong>Steps to change your studio handle:</strong></h2>
<ol>
 	<li> Go to your <strong><strong>Settings</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-267 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-01-004741.png" alt="" width="971" height="207" /></li>
 	<li>Click on the <strong>Account</strong> tab</li>
 	<li>Find the <strong>Studio Handle</strong> field<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-268 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-01-004806.png" alt="" width="970" height="349" /></li>
 	<li>Enter your new handle</li>
 	<li>Click <strong>Submit</strong></li>
</ol>
That’s it — your handle will be updated instantly!
