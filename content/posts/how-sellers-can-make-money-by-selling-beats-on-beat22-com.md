<b>Know about Beat22</b>

Founded in 2019 by Kanav Singh Mehra(CCO, Beat22.com) and Ashish Sudhera(CEO, Beat22.com) which is headquartered in Gurgaon, Haryana. Beat22 is Asia's largest online marketplace for buying and selling beats. Unlike Western platforms, Beat22 focuses exclusively on Asian music genres, including Hip-Hop, Punjabi Hip-Hop, Desi Hip-Hop, and Indian music. This niche focus allows producers to connect with a targeted audience of singers, rappers, and music artists seeking fresh, culturally resonant sounds.
