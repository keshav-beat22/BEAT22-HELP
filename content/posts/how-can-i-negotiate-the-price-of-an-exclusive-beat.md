<strong>Want a better deal?</strong>
You can easily negotiate the price, rights, and terms of any exclusive beat in 3 simple rounds built for artists like you.

<strong>STEP 1: START NEGOTIATION</strong>
Buyer can negotiate exclusive licenses only. By clicking on negotiate, one can easily start negotiating.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-287 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-134130.png" alt="" width="978" height="374" />

<strong>STEP 2: PROVIDE NEGOTIABLE TERMS</strong>
Provide the license fee, master, and publishing rights to proceed. A maximum of three negotiation rounds is allowed.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-288 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-134141.png" alt="" width="975" height="323" />

<strong>STEP 3: LOCK THE DEAL</strong>
After finalizing the deal and accepting the offer from the producer, the buyer has 24 hours to complete the purchase.
