Welcome to <strong>Beat22, </strong>your home for selling beats to artists, content creators, and music lovers worldwide. Uploading your first beat is super easy, and we’re here to walk you through the entire process like a friend.

Let’s get started!
<h3><strong>Step 1: Click “Upload” on the Top-Right</strong></h3>
Once you log in, look at the <strong>top-right corner</strong> of your screen.

Click the <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-152" src="/images/2025/06/upload.png" alt="" width="69" height="35" /> button to begin the process.

This opens the 3-step beat upload form.
<h3><strong>Step 2: Fill in Beat Metadata</strong></h3>
This is where you describe your beat, basically telling people what it sounds like.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-166 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-144023.png" alt="" width="1312" height="690" />

You’ll be asked to fill:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Beat Title</strong> – Give your beat a name</li>
 	<li><strong>Beat Type</strong> – Beat, Switch Beat or Beat With Hook
<ul>
 	<li style="list-style-type: none;">
<ul>
 	<li>
<p data-start="147" data-end="296"><strong data-start="147" data-end="162">Switch Beat</strong> in music means a beat or instrumental that contains <strong data-start="215" data-end="273">two (or more) different beats played one after another</strong> within the same track.</p>
<p data-start="298" data-end="311">It’s used to:</p>

<ul data-start="312" data-end="419">
 	<li data-start="312" data-end="339">
<p data-start="314" data-end="339"><strong data-start="314" data-end="339">Surprise the listener</strong></p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="340" data-end="378">
<p data-start="342" data-end="378">Keep the song <strong data-start="356" data-end="378">fresh and engaging</strong></p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="379" data-end="419">
<p data-start="381" data-end="419">Show off <strong data-start="390" data-end="405">versatility</strong> in production</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p data-start="421" data-end="538">Common in hip-hop and trap, the switch often happens during a <strong data-start="483" data-end="499">verse change</strong>, <strong data-start="501" data-end="514">hook drop</strong>, or <strong data-start="519" data-end="537">beat breakdown</strong>.</p>
<p data-start="540" data-end="632" data-is-last-node="" data-is-only-node="">In short:<br data-start="549" data-end="552" /><strong data-start="552" data-end="632" data-is-last-node="">Switch Beat = one track, two beats, back-to-back, to keep the vibe exciting.</strong></p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p data-start="0" data-end="129"><strong data-start="0" data-end="18">Beat with Hook</strong> means the instrumental <strong data-start="42" data-end="91">already includes a pre-recorded chorus (hook)</strong>—usually sung or rapped by a vocalist.</p>
<p data-start="131" data-end="144">This hook is:</p>

<ul data-start="145" data-end="304">
 	<li data-start="145" data-end="185">
<p data-start="147" data-end="185"><strong data-start="147" data-end="170">Catchy and repeated</strong>, like a chorus</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="186" data-end="247">
<p data-start="188" data-end="247">Placed in the beat so the artist can build verses around it</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="248" data-end="304">
<p data-start="250" data-end="304">Useful if you want a song structure that’s ready to go</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p data-start="306" data-end="370" data-is-last-node="" data-is-only-node="">In short:<br data-start="315" data-end="318" /><strong data-start="318" data-end="370" data-is-last-node="">Beat with Hook = instrumental + pre-made chorus.</strong></p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li><strong>Genre – </strong>Select the genre of the beat you're uploading, whether it's Trap, Drill, Boom Bap, etc.</li>
 	<li><strong>Instruments – </strong>Select the instruments used in your beat, such as piano, guitar, saxophone, etc.</li>
 	<li><strong>Mood</strong> – Choose the mood of your beat—chill, dark, aggressive, or anything else that fits</li>
 	<li><strong>Tempo</strong> – Select the tempo (BPM – Beats Per Minute) of your beat.</li>
 	<li><strong>Tags</strong> – Add tags (keywords) to help your beat get discovered by the right audience.</li>
</ul>
<p data-start="0" data-end="115">If your beat doesn't contain a sample, there's nothing to mention, just turn off the "<strong>Used Samples</strong>" button.<strong> But if it does</strong>, clearly state whether the sample is royalty-free or requires royalties.</p>
<p data-start="0" data-end="115"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-574" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-24-145700.png" alt="" width="1173" height="231" /></p>

<ul>
 	<li data-start="64" data-end="182">
<p data-start="66" data-end="182">Mention whether the sample is cleared or not. If the sample is cleared, check the box. If not, leave it unchecked.</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="183" data-end="273">
<p data-start="185" data-end="273">Provide the name of the sample owner so the buyer is aware before purchasing the beat.</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="183" data-end="273">
<p data-start="185" data-end="273">Add a link to the sample owner’s page, so the buyer knows where to go to clear the sample.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p data-start="293" data-end="356" data-is-last-node="" data-is-only-node="">This helps avoid copyright issues and builds trust with buyers.</p>
Click <strong>Next</strong> once you're done.
<h3><strong>Step 3: Upload Your Beat Media</strong></h3>
Here you’ll upload the actual files that make up your beat.

You’ll see 4 fields:

<strong>1. Tagged MP3 File</strong>

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-148 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/mp3.png" alt="" width="517" height="170" />
This is the version buyers will hear on the site, add your voice/beat tag here for protection. Size of the file should be min 2 mb and max 10 mb.

<strong>2. Cover Art</strong>

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-143 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/cover-art.png" alt="" width="510" height="167" />
Upload a square image to give your beat a professional look. Logo dimensions should be 220*220 and size should not exceed 2 mb.

<strong>3. Untagged WAV File</strong>

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-153 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/wav.png" alt="" width="525" height="171" />

This is the high-quality version sent after purchase. Upload a stereo 44.1 kHz WAV format file. Size should be atleast 10 mb to 300 mb.

<strong>4. Stem Files (.ZIP format)</strong>

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-150 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/stem.png" alt="" width="518" height="174" />
Upload all your beat elements (drums, melody, bass, etc.) as separate WAV files inside a zipped folder.

Click <strong>Next</strong> after uploading.

<strong>Important:</strong> Make sure your beat is mixed and exported properly. Poor audio = fewer sales!
<h3><strong>Step 4: Set License Terms &amp; Enable Mp3 Downloads</strong></h3>
Here’s where you<span style="font-size: 16px;"> define </span><strong style="font-size: 16px;">how your beat can be used</strong><span style="font-size: 16px;"> and </span><strong style="font-size: 16px;">how much it costs</strong><span style="font-size: 16px;">.</span>

<strong>1. Enable MP3 Down</strong><strong style="font-size: 16px;">load</strong><span style="font-size: 16px;"> (optional)</span>
<h3 style="font-style: normal;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-169 size-full" style="font-weight: inherit; font-size: 16px;" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-144740.png" alt="" width="960" height="104" /></h3>
<ul>
 	<li style="list-style-type: none;">
<ul>
 	<li>If you want to let users preview your beat fully before they buy.<br data-start="527" data-end="530" />Just toggle this ON to allow free MP3 downloads.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<strong>2. Non-Exclusive License</strong>
<p style="padding-left: 40px; text-align: left;" data-start="0" data-end="115">A <strong data-start="2" data-end="27">Non-Exclusive License</strong> means the <strong data-start="38" data-end="64">beat is not only yours, </strong>the producer can <strong data-start="82" data-end="114">sell it to other artists too</strong>.</p>
<p style="padding-left: 40px; text-align: left;" data-start="117" data-end="146">You have legal permission to:</p>

<ul style="text-align: left;">
 	<li style="list-style-type: none;">
<ul data-start="147" data-end="227">
 	<li data-start="147" data-end="174">
<p data-start="149" data-end="174">Use the beat in your song</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="175" data-end="227">
<p data-start="177" data-end="227">Distribute and monetize it (within license limits)</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p style="padding-left: 40px; text-align: left;" data-start="229" data-end="233">But:</p>

<ul>
 	<li style="list-style-type: none;">
<ul data-start="234" data-end="322">
 	<li style="text-align: left;" data-start="234" data-end="262">
<p data-start="236" data-end="262"><strong data-start="236" data-end="262">You don’t own the beat</strong></p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="234" data-end="262">
<p style="text-align: left;" data-start="236" data-end="262"><strong data-start="265" data-end="297">Others can use the same beat</strong> under their own licenses</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
This allows <strong>multiple buyers</strong> to use the same beat.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-679" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2026-03-11-115239.png" alt="" width="953" height="514" />

&nbsp;
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Set the license fee</strong> for WAV, and WAV + STEM options</li>
 	<li> Licensing Terms:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Publishing Rights %</strong>
<ul>
 	<li>
<p data-start="0" data-end="123"><strong data-start="0" data-end="21">What are Publishing rights?</strong></p>

<ul>
 	<li>
<p data-start="0" data-end="123">Publishing rights in the music industry refer to the rights over the <strong data-start="73" data-end="104">songwriting and composition</strong>—not the recording.</p>
<p data-start="125" data-end="136">This means:</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="138" data-end="247">
<p data-start="140" data-end="247">Whoever wrote the <strong data-start="158" data-end="168">lyrics</strong> and <strong data-start="173" data-end="183">melody</strong> (like a songwriter or composer) owns the <strong data-start="225" data-end="246">publishing rights</strong>.</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="248" data-end="385">
<p data-start="250" data-end="385">These rights let them earn money when the song is played on radio, streamed, used in films, covered by someone else, or performed live.</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="386" data-end="480">
<p data-start="388" data-end="480">Music publishers help manage these rights and collect royalties on behalf of the songwriter.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li><strong>Number of Master Recordings</strong>
<ul>
 	<li>
<p data-start="0" data-end="146"><strong data-start="0" data-end="57">"Number of Master Recordings"</strong> means how many <strong data-start="73" data-end="104">different songs or versions</strong> you’re allowed to create using that beat.</p>
<p data-start="148" data-end="160">For example:</p>

<ul data-start="161" data-end="391">
 	<li data-start="161" data-end="254">
<p data-start="163" data-end="254">If the license says <strong data-start="183" data-end="205">1 master recording</strong>, you can make <strong data-start="220" data-end="238">one final song</strong> with that beat.</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="255" data-end="391">
<p data-start="257" data-end="391">If it allows <strong data-start="270" data-end="293">multiple recordings</strong>, you can create <strong data-start="310" data-end="339">several songs or versions</strong> (like remixes, features, etc.) using the same beat.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p data-start="393" data-end="478" data-is-last-node="" data-is-only-node="">In short: it tells you how many finished songs you’re allowed to make using the beat.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li><strong>License Period</strong>
<ul>
 	<li>
<p data-start="0" data-end="117"><strong data-start="0" data-end="30">License period</strong> means how long you’re allowed to legally use the beat under the license you purchased.</p>
<p data-start="119" data-end="131">For example:</p>

<ul data-start="132" data-end="338">
 	<li data-start="132" data-end="243">
<p data-start="134" data-end="243">If the license period is <strong data-start="159" data-end="169">1 year</strong>, you can use the beat in your song for <strong data-start="209" data-end="219">1 year</strong> from the purchase date.</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="244" data-end="338">
<p data-start="246" data-end="338">After that, you'd need to <strong data-start="272" data-end="281">renew</strong> or <strong data-start="285" data-end="296">upgrade</strong> the license to continue using it legally.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p data-start="340" data-end="404" data-is-last-node="" data-is-only-node="">In short: <strong data-start="350" data-end="404" data-is-last-node="">It’s the time limit on your right to use the beat.</strong></p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<strong>3. Exclusive License</strong>
<p style="padding-left: 40px;" data-start="0" data-end="162">An <strong data-start="3" data-end="24">Exclusive License</strong> means <strong data-start="31" data-end="81">you’re the only artist allowed to use the beat</strong>—once it’s sold to you, the producer <strong data-start="118" data-end="161">can’t sell or license it to anyone else</strong>.</p>
<p style="padding-left: 40px;" data-start="164" data-end="172">You can:</p>

<ul>
 	<li style="list-style-type: none;">
<ul data-start="173" data-end="324">
 	<li data-start="173" data-end="206">
<p data-start="175" data-end="206">Use the beat in unlimited songs</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="207" data-end="265">
<p data-start="209" data-end="265">Distribute, monetize, and perform it without competition</p>
</li>
 	<li data-start="266" data-end="324">
<p data-start="268" data-end="324">Often get more rights and flexibility than non-exclusive</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p data-start="326" data-end="401" data-is-last-node="" data-is-only-node="">In short: <strong data-start="336" data-end="401" data-is-last-node="">You get full control of the beat, and no one else can use it.</strong></p>
This allows <strong>only one person</strong> to own the beat.

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-680" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2026-03-11-115435.png" alt="" width="970" height="426" />

&nbsp;

Fill in:
<ol>
 	<li>Set the <strong>license fee of the Exclusive License</strong></li>
 	<li>Licensing Terms:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Publishing Rights %</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>License Period</strong> = Lifetime</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<strong>Note:</strong> Once a beat is sold exclusively, it will be removed from the store.
<h3><strong>Step 5: Enable Negotiation Feature (Highly Recommended)</strong></h3>
<p data-start="124" data-end="194"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-569" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-24-143459-300x79.png" alt="" width="300" height="79" /></p>
<p data-start="124" data-end="194">This is one of the most powerful tools available to sellers on Beat22.</p>
<p data-start="196" data-end="350">Make sure to turn on the Negotiation feature for beats where the <strong data-start="261" data-end="282">Exclusive License</strong> is enabled, it increases your chances of closing high-value deals.</p>
<strong>Why?</strong>
Sellers who enable negotiation to see up to <strong>30% more sales</strong>. It gives buyers flexibility to offer a price instead of walking away.

<strong>Final Step: Acknowledge the Terms</strong>

Before submitting, check the box confirming:
<ul>
 	<li>You’re not uploading beats with <strong>pornographic</strong> or <strong>hate/seditious</strong> content</li>
 	<li>All content is either original or properly cleared (especially if using samples)</li>
</ul>
Click <strong>Submit</strong>.

Once submitted, your beat will go live.
