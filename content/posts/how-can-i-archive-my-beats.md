Sometimes you may want to temporarily remove a beat from public view without permanently deleting it. That’s where the Archive feature comes in handy.

Archiving a beat means it's hidden from the marketplace, but you can still access it privately from your dashboard and restore it anytime.
<h3>How to Archive Your Beat on Beat22:</h3>
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Go to Your Dashboard</strong>
Log into your Beat22 seller account and head over to your dashboard.</li>
 	<li><strong><strong>Click on Browse at the top</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-190 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-210101.png" alt="" width="1096" height="118" /></li>
 	<li><strong style="font-size: 16px;"><strong style="font-size: 16px;">Click on “My Beats”</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-246 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-01-001344.png" alt="" width="1203" height="355" /></li>
 	<li><strong style="font-size: 16px;"><strong style="font-size: 16px;">Click on the “Active” Button</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-247 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-01-001400.png" alt="" width="1198" height="243" /></li>
 	<li><strong style="font-size: 16px;"><strong style="font-size: 16px;">A pop-up will appear, select “Yes” to confirm. Your beat will be archived.</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-248 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-01-001412.png" alt="" width="1200" height="438" /></li>
</ol>
