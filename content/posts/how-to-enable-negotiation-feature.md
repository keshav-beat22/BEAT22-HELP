Only Exclusive License can be Negotiated.
<h3><strong>You can turn on the Negotiation by either applying the Global Rule or the Local Rule</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Global Rule</strong> helps you to turn on the negotiation on all the beats (except the ones having local rule.)</li>
 	<li><strong>Local Rule</strong> helps you to to turn on the negotiation on a specific beat</li>
</ul>
<strong>Tip</strong>: Local rule is prioritized over the Global rule.
<h3><strong>How to enable the Global rule?</strong></h3>
Turn it ON for all from <strong>Studio Settings &gt; Studio Controls</strong>

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-207 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-214306.png" alt="" width="1014" height="321" />
<h3><strong>How to enable the Local Rule?</strong></h3>
<strong>1) While Uploading</strong>: Turn on the button "Is it negotiable?"

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-199 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-211956.png" alt="" width="1024" height="277" />

<strong>2) After Uploading:</strong>
<ul>
 	<li>Go to <strong>My Beats</strong></li>
</ul>
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-200 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-212015.png" alt="" width="1041" height="314" />
<ul>
 	<li>Click the icon on any exclusive beat
<em>Note: The Negotiation feature can only be enabled if the Exclusive License option is turned on for the beat.</em></li>
</ul>
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-201 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-212042.png" alt="" width="1020" height="262" />
