Bought a beat on Beat22 but received a file with a voice tag? Don’t worry, we’ve got you covered.

Here’s why it might happen and how to quickly get it resolved.
<h3><strong>Why does this happen?</strong></h3>
Each beat on Beat22 is <strong>uploaded and managed directly by the producer</strong>. Sometimes, the seller may <strong>accidentally upload a tagged version</strong> instead of the untagged one meant for licensed buyers.

Beat22 does <strong>not alter, tag, or reprocess</strong> beat files, we simply deliver what the seller uploads.
<h3><strong>What to do If you received a tagged file:</strong></h3>
If your purchased beat still contains a voice tag, follow these steps:
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Take a screenshot</strong> of your payment confirmation or invoice</li>
 	<li>Note down the <strong>beat name and order number</strong></li>
 	<li>Email us at <strong>support@beat22.com</strong> with the details</li>
</ol>
Once we receive your email, we’ll coordinate with the seller and send you the correct <strong>untagged file</strong> as soon as possible.
<h3><strong>Why can’t Beat22 just fix it directly?</strong></h3>
Beat22 is a <strong>marketplace,</strong> the content is fully controlled by the sellers. We don't modify or replace beat files unless the seller provides a corrected version. However, we’re always here to support you in such situations and ensure you get what you paid for.

<strong>Tip: Always Check File Type Before Using Publicly</strong>

If anything seems off with your download (e.g. tags, missing stems, or wrong file format), let us know early. We’re happy to help.
