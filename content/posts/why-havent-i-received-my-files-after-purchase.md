Just purchased a beat on Beat22 but didn’t get your download files? Don’t worry, it’s usually a simple issue, and we’ll help you get your files quickly.

Here’s what might have happened and how to fix it:

<strong>1. Check Your Email</strong>

As soon as you complete your purchase, we automatically send your:
<ul>
 	<li>Beat file (MP3 / WAV / Stems)</li>
 	<li>License agreement PDF</li>
 	<li>Invoice and receipt</li>
</ul>
<strong>Check your inbox and spam/junk folder</strong> for an email from <strong>Beat22</strong>.

<strong>2. Check “My Orders” in Your Account</strong>

You can always access your purchased files directly from your Beat22 account:

<strong>Steps:</strong>
<ol>
 	<li>Log in to your account at <a href="https://beat22.com">beat22.com</a></li>
 	<li>Click <strong>“Browse”</strong> at the top</li>
 	<li>Select <strong>“My Orders”</strong> from the dropdown</li>
 	<li>Click on your purchased beat</li>
 	<li>Download your media file, license, and receipt</li>
</ol>
<strong>3. Order Still Showing “Pending”?</strong>

If your order status still shows <strong>“Pending”</strong>, it means your payment hasn’t fully processed yet. In this case, wait a few minutes, most issues resolve within a short time.

If it remains pending after 10–15 minutes, contact us.

<strong>4. Still Not Received Anything?</strong>

Sometimes there may be:
<ul>
 	<li>A <strong>network delay</strong></li>
 	<li>A <strong>technical error</strong></li>
 	<li>Or a rare case where payment went through but the files didn’t trigger</li>
</ul>
Just email us at <strong>support@beat22.com</strong> with:
<ul>
 	<li>A <strong>screenshot of your payment</strong></li>
 	<li>Your <strong>order number</strong></li>
 	<li>The <strong>name of the beat</strong></li>
</ul>
We’ll verify and send your files manually ASAP.

<strong>Our Promise</strong>

We want every buyer to have a smooth experience. If something goes wrong, we’ll fix it fast and make sure you get everything you paid for.
