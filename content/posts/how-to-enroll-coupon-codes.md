Want to boost your beat sales with special offers? Beat22 has made it easy. You can enroll your beats into official Beat22 coupons that we’ve already curated.
<h3><strong>What are coupon codes on Beat22?</strong></h3>
Coupon Codes allow you to offer discounts on your beats, helping drive more sales, attract new buyers, and reward loyal fans.
<h3><strong>How to enroll and use coupon codes:</strong></h3>
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Go to Your Dashboard</strong>
Log into your Beat22 seller account and head over to your dashboard. <strong><strong>Click on Browse at the top</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-190 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-210101.png" alt="" width="1096" height="118" /></li>
 	<li><strong><strong>Click on Coupons</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-191 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-210115.png" alt="" width="1094" height="281" /></li>
 	<li><strong><strong><strong>Click on whichever coupon you want to enroll</strong></strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-192 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-210127.png" alt="" width="1092" height="237" />&nbsp;</li>
 	<li><strong><strong><strong>Select the expiry date and choose the beat you want to offer discount on from your catalogue</strong></strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-193 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-210141.png" alt="" width="1090" height="329" />&nbsp;</li>
 	<li><strong><strong>Click on “Enroll Coupon”</strong></strong></li>
 	<li><strong><strong>Once the coupon is enrolled, you'll see a coupon icon next to your beat name.</strong></strong></li>
</ol>
