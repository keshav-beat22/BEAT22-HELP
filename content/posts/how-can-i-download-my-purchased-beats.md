Bought a beat and ready to download your files? Beat22 makes it quick and easy to access all your purchased beats, licenses, and invoices right from your account.

Follow this simple guide to download your beats anytime.
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Log in to your Beat22 account</strong>
Visit <a href="https://beat22.com">beat22.com</a> and sign in with your buyer account.</li>
 	<li><strong>Click on “Browse” at the top</strong>
Once logged in, you’ll see the <strong>“Browse”</strong> button at the top of the page. Click on it.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-432 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-07-163327.png" alt="" width="1256" height="144" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Go to “Your Orders”</strong>
In the dropdown, click on <strong>“Your Orders”</strong>. This will open your full order history.
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-676" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2026-02-14-152523.png" alt="" width="754" height="162" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Select the Beat You Purchased</strong>
Find the beat you’ve purchased from the list and click on it.</li>
 	<li><strong>Download Your Files</strong>
From the beat’s order details page, you’ll be able to:</li>
</ol>
<ul>
 	<li> Download the <strong>media file</strong> (MP3/WAV/Stems)</li>
 	<li> Download the <strong>license PDF</strong></li>
 	<li> Access your <strong>invoice and payment receipt
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-672" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2026-02-14-150018.png" alt="" width="1675" height="382" />
</strong></li>
</ul>
<strong>Quick Tip:</strong>

Your files will always remain available in your account under “My Orders.” You can return any time to re-download if needed.
