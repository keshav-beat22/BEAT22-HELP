<p style="text-align: left;">Uploading beats on Beat22 helps you reach thousands of artists. Here are quick answers to common questions about the upload process. Whether you're a new seller or just need a quick refresher, this guide will help you upload confidently and correctly.</p>

<ol style="text-align: left;">
 	<li>
<h3><strong> How do I upload a beat on Beat22 Studio?</strong></h3>
</li>
</ol>
<p style="text-align: left;">Uploading a beat is simple. Just log in to your seller account and <strong>click on the <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-152" src="/images/2025/06/upload.png" alt="" width="74" height="38" /> button at the top right of the page</strong>. You’ll be guided through a step-by-step process covering:</p>

<ul style="text-align: left;">
 	<li>Metadata entry (Beat title, type, genre, instruments, mood, tempo, tags, etc.)</li>
 	<li>Media upload (Tagged MP3, Cover Art, Untagged WAV, and Stems in ZIP format)</li>
 	<li>License and pricing</li>
 	<li>Optional settings like enabling negotiations for exclusive beats</li>
</ul>
<p style="text-align: left;">For a detailed walkthrough, read our full article: <a href="/how-to-upload-beats/">/how-to-upload-beats/</a></p>

<ol style="text-align: left;" start="2">
 	<li>
<h3><strong> What audio file formats are supported?</strong></h3>
</li>
</ol>
<p style="text-align: left;">Beat22 supports the following file types:</p>

<ul style="text-align: left;">
 	<li>Tagged MP3</li>
 	<li>WAV</li>
 	<li>WAV + STEMS (.ZIP Format)</li>
</ul>
<ol style="text-align: left;" start="3">
 	<li>
<h3><strong>Should I upload stems with or without effects?</strong></h3>
</li>
</ol>
<p style="text-align: left;">This depends on your production style and the needs of potential buyers.</p>

<ul style="text-align: left;">
 	<li>Generally recommended: Upload stems without effects, so artists and engineers have full flexibility while mixing.</li>
 	<li>Exception: If an effect is crucial to the beat's vibe, you may choose to leave it on.</li>
</ul>
<p style="text-align: left;">Use your best judgment based on what an artist or their engineer would expect from a professional stem pack.</p>

<ol style="text-align: left;" start="4">
 	<li>
<h3><strong> What are Beat Tags and why do they matter?</strong></h3>
</li>
</ol>
<p style="text-align: left;">Beat tags are keywords (similar to hashtags) that help categorize your beat and make it more discoverable on the marketplace. Tags can describe:</p>

<ul style="text-align: left;">
 	<li>Genre (e.g., #Trap, #Drill, #LoFi)</li>
 	<li>Mood (e.g., #Dark, #Uplifting)</li>
 	<li>Instrument (e.g., #Piano, #808)</li>
 	<li>Style or influence (e.g., #DrakeTypeBeat, #IndianDrill)</li>
</ul>
<p style="text-align: left;">Beat22’s search algorithm uses these tags to match beats with artist preferences. The right tags can significantly boost your visibility and sales. So, tag wisely!</p>

<h3><strong>Final Tip: Upload with Intent</strong></h3>
<p style="text-align: left;">Uploading isn’t just about getting your beat online. It’s about presenting it professionally from accurate metadata to clean stems and strategic tags. This gives buyers a great first impression and increases your chances of making a sale.</p>
<p style="text-align: left;">If you’re serious about growing your brand as a music producer, take the time to upload properly.</p>
