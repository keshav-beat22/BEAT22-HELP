Yes, you can absolutely edit the price of any license. Whether you want to adjust pricing based on demand, experiment with new strategies, or offer a temporary drop. You’re in full control.

Here’s how it works:

<strong>How to Edit Your License Price</strong>
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Log into your Beat22 seller account</strong></li>
 	<li><strong><strong>Click on Browse at the top</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-190 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-210101.png" alt="" width="1096" height="118" /></li>
 	<li><strong><strong>Click on “My Beats”</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-200 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-212015.png" alt="" width="1041" height="314" /></li>
 	<li><strong><strong>Click on Edit option [circled]</strong></strong><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-259 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-07-01-003446.png" alt="" width="970" height="142" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Scroll to the License Section</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>Update the price for any license (Non-Exclusive and Exclusive)</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>Click “Submit”</strong></li>
</ol>
Your new prices will be reflected instantly on the marketplace.
