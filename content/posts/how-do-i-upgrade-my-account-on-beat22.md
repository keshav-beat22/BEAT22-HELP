Whether you're just starting out or looking to unlock full earning potential, upgrading your Beat22 account is easy and flexible. Here's a breakdown of your upgrade options based on your current plan.
<h2><strong>If you're on the basic plan:</strong></h2>
You can upgrade anytime by going to the <strong>Subscriptions</strong> section in your dashboard or by clicking on this link: <a href="https://beat22.com/subscriptions">Subscriptions | beat22</a>

<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-225 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-234219.png" alt="" width="1111" height="132" />
<ul>
 	<li>You can choose from:</li>
</ul>
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Premium Annual – ₹3,999/year</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>Pro Monthly – ₹599/month</strong></li>
 	<li><strong>Pro Annual – ₹5,999/year</strong> <em>(Best value!)</em></li>
</ol>
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-226 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-234303.png" alt="" width="922" height="536" />
<ul>
 	<li>Then click on “Continue to payment”</li>
</ul>
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-227 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-234345.png" alt="" width="928" height="289" />
<ul>
 	<li>Fill in your contact details, email ID and proceed with the payment via Amazon Pay, PhonePe, GooglePay, UPI, Credit Card, Debit card:</li>
</ul>
<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-228 size-full" src="/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-30-234401.png" alt="" width="831" height="568" />
<ul>
 	<li>Click the checkbox to agree to the “I agree to Terms of Service and Privacy Policy”.</li>
 	<li>Click "Pay &amp; Subscribe" to finalize the upgrade.</li>
</ul>
<strong>Why go Pro Annual?</strong>
<ul>
 	<li>Top placement in searches &amp; playlists</li>
 	<li>Unlock negotiation for exclusive beats</li>
 	<li>Maximum earning potential</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>If You’re on the Premium Annual Plan:</strong></h2>
You have 3 options:
<ol>
 	<li>
<h3><strong> Let it complete</strong></h3>
</li>
</ol>
<ul>
 	<li>If you’ve canceled auto-renew (mandate), your plan will end after one year.</li>
 	<li>You won’t be charged again unless you manually renew.</li>
</ul>
<em>Please email us at support@beat22.com to cancel the mandate.</em>
<ol start="2">
 	<li>
<h3><strong> Upgrade to pro monthly (Midway)</strong></h3>
</li>
</ol>
<ul>
 	<li>Pay ₹599 separately for that month.</li>
 	<li>Your Premium plan will continue in parallel.</li>
</ul>
<em>Please email us at <strong>support@beat22.com</strong> to upgrade your plan to Pro monthly.</em>
<ol start="3">
 	<li>
<h3><strong> Upgrade to pro annual (Midway)</strong></h3>
</li>
</ol>
You have <strong>two choices</strong>:
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Switch and Adjust</strong>: Pay an adjusted amount for the remaining months based on your usage so far. Send us an email to supportbeat22.com for upgradation.</li>
 	<li><strong>Start Fresh</strong>: Cancel Premium and begin a <strong>new Pro Annual plan</strong> by paying ₹5,999.</li>
</ul>
<em>Please email us at support@beat22.com to upgrade your plan to Pro Annual.</em>

<strong>We recommend starting fresh with Pro Annual</strong> for uninterrupted benefits for a full year.
<h2><strong>If you’re on the pro monthly plan:</strong></h2>
Want to upgrade to <strong>Pro Annual</strong>?
<ul>
 	<li>You can do so anytime by paying ₹5,999.</li>
 	<li>Your current monthly payment won’t be refunded.</li>
 	<li>A new Pro Annual cycle will begin immediately.</li>
</ul>
Best for serious producers who want consistency, full features, and no monthly hassle.
