At Beat22, we’ve made the licensing process <strong>fully automatic,</strong> so you can focus on selling beats, not chasing paperwork.

Here’s how it works:

<strong>Step 1: Buyer Purchases Your Beat</strong>

Once a buyer completes the checkout process, the system instantly captures the details of the transaction, beat name, license type, price, and buyer info.

<strong>Step 2: License &amp; Contract Auto-Generated</strong>

Right after the payment, Beat22’s system <strong>automatically creates a legally binding license agreement</strong> between you (the seller) and the buyer.

No manual work
No separate contract needed
License includes all terms, usage rights, publishing %, master rights, etc.

<strong>Step 3: Delivered to Buyer + You</strong>

The finalized license is sent instantly to the buyer via email and is also available for both parties to download from their Beat22 dashboard.

<strong>Why This Matters for You as a Seller:</strong>
<ul>
 	<li>You <strong>don’t need to draft or upload</strong> any contract manually</li>
 	<li>The license is <strong>professional and legally valid</strong></li>
 	<li>You can <strong>focus on music</strong> while we take care of the business side</li>
</ul>
<strong>Beat sold. License handled. Contract ready, all in seconds.</strong>
