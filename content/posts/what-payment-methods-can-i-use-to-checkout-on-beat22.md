Here are the payment methods you can use:
<h3><strong>In India:</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>UPI</strong> (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)</li>
 	<li><strong>Credit/Debit Cards</strong> (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay)</li>
 	<li><strong>Net Banking</strong> (All major banks supported)</li>
 	<li><strong>Wallets</strong> (Paytm, Mobikwik, Freecharge)</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>For buyers outside India:</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>PayPal</strong> – Secure and widely accepted</li>
 	<li><strong>International Debit/Credit Cards</strong> (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Quick tips:</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li>All payments are <strong>100% secure and encrypted</strong>.</li>
 	<li>You’ll get an <strong>instant download</strong> and <strong>license certificate</strong> after payment.</li>
 	<li>If a payment fails, try another method or reach out to support@beat22.com.</li>
</ul>
