Found a beat you love and want to share it with your friends, artist, or producer? Beat22 makes it super easy to share beats directly from the platform in just a few clicks.

Here’s how you can do it:
<h3><strong>Step-by-Step: How to Share a Beat</strong></h3>
<ol>
 	<li><strong>Go to </strong><a href="https://beat22.com"><strong>beat22.com</strong></a>
Browse the platform and find the beat you want to share.<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-364 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-190959.png" alt="" width="1056" height="172" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Click on the 3 Vertical Dots (</strong><strong>⋮)</strong>
On the beat card (top right corner), click the <strong>three vertical dots</strong> next to the price.<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-365 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-191008.png" alt="" width="1059" height="166" /></li>
 	<li><strong>Select “Share”</strong>
From the dropdown menu, click on <strong>“Share”</strong>.</li>
 	<li><strong>Copy the Link or Use Social Options</strong></li>
</ol>
<ul>
 	<li>A <strong>link will be copied</strong> to your clipboard, which you can paste anywhere (WhatsApp, Instagram DM, email, etc.)</li>
 	<li>Or you may see <strong>direct social sharing options</strong> like Twitter, Facebook, etc.<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-366 size-full" src="/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-01-191019.png" alt="" width="1056" height="380" /></li>
</ul>
<strong>Now What?</strong>

Once you’ve shared the beat link:
<ul>
 	<li>The recipient can <strong>preview the beat</strong></li>
 	<li>They can <strong>see pricing and licensing options</strong></li>
 	<li>If they like it, they can buy or make an offer right away!</li>
</ul>
<strong>Use It To:</strong>
<ul>
 	<li>Share beat options with artists or collaborators</li>
 	<li>Show your team before purchasing</li>
 	<li>Recommend a beat to your friends or followers</li>
</ul>
