Looking to try out a beat before committing to a license? Beat22 offers a <strong>Free Download</strong> option on select beats so you can explore, write, and vibe <strong>risk-free</strong>.

But what exactly can you do with a free download? Here’s everything you need to know:
<h2><strong>What Is a Free Download?</strong></h2>
A <strong>Free Download</strong> gives you access to a tagged version of the beat for <strong>non-commercial use only</strong>. It’s meant to help you <strong>try before you buy</strong>.
<h3><strong>What You <em>Can</em> Do:</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Listen &amp; Write</strong> to the beat privately</li>
 	<li><strong>Practice, record demo vocals</strong>, or draft your verse</li>
 	<li><strong>Plan your project</strong> before deciding which license to buy</li>
 	<li>Share ideas with your team, manager, or collaborators</li>
 	<li>Use it to test <strong>vibe, flow, and fit</strong> before purchasing</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>What You <em>Can’t</em> Do:</strong></h3>
<ul>
 	<li><strong>Release</strong> the song publicly (YouTube, Spotify, etc.)</li>
 	<li><strong>Monetize</strong> the track in any way</li>
 	<li>Use the beat in <strong>music videos, ads, or performances</strong></li>
 	<li>Remove tags or claim ownership of the beat</li>
 	<li>Use it for <strong>commercial gain</strong> or publishing</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>Why Use a Free Download?</strong></h2>
<ul>
 	<li>Try out multiple beats before buying</li>
 	<li>Create solid demo drafts before investing</li>
 	<li>Shortlist beats with your team or label</li>
 	<li>Boost your confidence in picking the right license</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>Ready to Go Public?</strong></h2>
Once your draft is ready and you’re confident, <strong>buy a license</strong> (WAV, WAV + Stems or Exclusive) to release your track legally and start monetizing it.

For any questions about licensing or how to buy, feel free to reach us at <strong>support@beat22.com</strong>
